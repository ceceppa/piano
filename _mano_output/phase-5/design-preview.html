What each view shows

Chord view

View · root · inversion selectors. Chord notes only in Understand. “Explore G chord types” only — the scale list is hidden and its selection kept. Playback: Hear G7 / Arpeggiate / Chord options.

Scale view

No inversion selector. Understand shows scale notes and never any chord content, on screen or to a screen reader. “Explore G scales” only. Playback: Hear G major / Play descending.

Both view

Everything above, plus the shared-notes row and the shared-note triangle marker. Both lists stack below the piano, chord types first, every supported option visible.
